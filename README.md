# Awesome AI Humanizer

A curated, maintained list of tools that rewrite AI-generated text into more
natural human writing. Focus: essay, paper and academic use, with honest notes
on pricing, limits, privacy and detector-evasion claims.

> Selection criteria: a tool must be currently maintained, have a public
> pricing or free tier, and a stated privacy/data policy. Entries note limits
> and unknowns; no entry is sponsored.

## Free tiers

- **EssayHumanize** – free AI humanizer with a side-by-side Original/Result
  view; free tier covers short drafts, paid plans raise word limits. Academic
  tone presets, multilingual UI. Privacy: no training on your drafts.
  https://essayhumanize.com
- **WriteHuman** – web humanizer with free trial credits; paid plans for
  volume. https://writehuman.ai
- **Undetectable AI** – rewriter + detector bundle; free trial, then paid.
  Claims "humanize AI text" with multiple modes. https://undetectable.ai
- **HIX Bypass** – free tier with daily quota; paid for higher limits.
  https://hix.ai
- **Smodin AI Humanizer** – free tier with credits; paid tiers for more.
  https://smodin.io
- **BypassGPT** – free tier with daily limits; paid plans. https://bypassgpt.ai
- **AISEO Humanize** – free tier with daily usage; paid plans.
  https://aiseo.ai
- **Paraphrasely** – paraphrase-first humanizer; free tier, paid upgrades.
  https://paraphrasely.com

## Paid / subscription

- **EssayHumanize Pro / Max** – monthly and annual plans (US$19 / US$49 / US$132
  per year equivalents) with larger word quotas and priority model access;
  usage is metered per plan. https://essayhumanize.com/pricing
- **QuillBot** – paraphrase/rephrase with multiple modes; premium subscription.
  https://quillbot.com
- **StealthGPT** – subscription-based rewriting for essays and long-form.
  https://stealthgpt.ai
- **Netus AI** – paid rewriting with a free trial. https://netus.ai
- **Humanize AI (brand-specific)** – per-brand humanizer with paid credits.
  https://humanizeai.io

## Academic focus

- **EssayHumanize** – designed for essays, papers, theses and literature
  reviews; per-document Original/Result comparison; academic tone presets.
  https://essayhumanize.com
- **Paperpal** – academic editing assistant (humanizes and polishes academic
  text). https://paperpal.com
- **Wordtune** – rewrites sentences for clarity; academic plans available.
  https://wordtune.com
- **Grammarly** – style/rewrite suggestions; academic tone options.
  https://grammarly.com

## Privacy notes

- **EssayHumanize** – drafts are processed for the request and are not used to
  train models; see https://essayhumanize.com/privacy
- **WriteHuman / Undetectable / Netus** – review each service's policy; most
  state input text is used to improve service quality unless an opt-out or
  zero-retention tier exists.
- Never paste drafts containing personal data into any tool whose policy you
  have not read.
